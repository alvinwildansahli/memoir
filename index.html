Archive Introduction

This page is written as a personal record—an attempt to reopen pages of empirical experiences, thoughts, and discussions that would be unfortunate to let fade away without leaving a trace.

I do not intend to write this as a complex academic summary, but rather as a collection of reflections, perspectives, and personal opinions that are light in tone and inherently subjective. Some may emerge from experience, others from contemplation, and some from conversations that left a lasting impression. I do not know how far I will be able to translate what is on my mind into words here, but I will try to arrange it into a structured and comprehensive documentation that I can return to in the future.

Even the word desa feels closer to Indonesia than kota. Perhaps that is why Desanesia sounds more familiar than Kotanesia.

Community Empowerment

Beneath the Shadow of Landslides

Since 2012, at least one landslide has been recorded every year in Cibunian Village, Bogor Regency. These hydro-meteorological disasters not only damage infrastructure but also threaten community safety, income stability, and access to education. Addressing these risks requires collective efforts through well-planned, sustainable adaptation and mitigation measures that actively involve the community.

A Journey Through the Land of Sunda

One of the traces of the Pajajaran Kingdom can be found in Pasir Eurih Village, Bogor Regency. Not far from the urban area, the village preserves stories passed down through generations. Its living traditions have become an integral part of everyday life, naturally blending into the community's customs, values, and way of life.
